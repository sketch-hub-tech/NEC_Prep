# NEC Computer Engineering — Complete Study Notes (ACtE Syllabus)

**Exam:** Nepal Engineering Council General Registration Examination
**Format:** 100 objective questions × 1 mark, pass 50, 2 hours, no negative marking
**Distribution:** 10 chapters × 10 questions; each chapter has 6 sub-chapters with ≥1 question each.

## How to use this pack
1. Read one chapter file per day (or per 2 days), in order.
2. Every sub-chapter (1.1 → 10.6) appears below — the guaranteed-question targets.
3. After each chapter, drill 30–50 MCQs on the free platforms (necexamhelper.com, neclicenseexam.dollarboysushil.com).
4. Last 2 weeks: full-length timed mocks + revise the "Bonus/Formula Boxes" inside each file.
